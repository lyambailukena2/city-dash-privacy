City Dash Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026
Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy describes information processed by City Dash, an Android game, based on the app implementation and third-party SDK configuration reviewed for this version.

1. Publisher and contact

City Dash is published by Lyamba Ilukena.

For privacy questions or requests, contact lyambailukena2@gmail.com.

2. Information processed by the app

The app stores the audio setting, best score, and virtual-coin balance locally on the device using Android app preferences. Gameplay values such as the current run's score are used while playing. The inspected code contains no app account, sign-in, or custom backend feature, and no implementation that sends these game values to a publisher-operated server.

The app uses Google Mobile Ads to request and display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google's current Google Play Data safety disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Legacy) describes automatic collection and sharing of IP address; user product interactions, including app launches, taps, and video views; diagnostic information about app and SDK performance; and device or account identifiers, including Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, and account-related identifiers where applicable. Google states these data are used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data handled for a particular user can depend on Google's services, device settings, and applicable regional settings.

This project uses the google_mobile_ads Flutter plugin version 9.1.0. Its default Android dependency in the inspected plugin build configuration is Google Play services Ads SDK 25.4.0. Google's currently published Data safety disclosure describes SDK version 25.5.0, so the publisher should recheck the disclosure for the exact SDK included in the final release and update this policy if the data practices differ. See Google's Mobile Ads SDK Data safety disclosure.

The Android manifest requests internet and network-state permissions for network operation and advertising. It does not request Android location permissions, and the inspected app code does not use device location APIs for any game feature. However, Google documents that the Mobile Ads SDK collects an IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location. Therefore, City Dash does not use device location as a game feature, but location may be inferred by Google's ad service from network information. See Google's Privacy Policy for Google's explanation of its data practices.

The inspected dependency list and app code show no Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, separate analytics SDK, location SDK, or publisher backend. Other included libraries provide game rendering, audio playback, and local preference storage; the reviewed code does not show them transmitting game data to a separate service. This describes the reviewed project configuration and may change if dependencies or app features change.

3. How information is used

Local game information is used to operate gameplay and preserve the player's audio choice, best score, and coin balance between app sessions. Information processed by Google Mobile Ads is used by Google and its advertising services to provide and operate ads, which may include ad delivery, measurement, security, and diagnostics according to Google's settings and policies.

Rewarded ads can grant 25 virtual coins in the game. Virtual coins have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for money.

4. Sharing and third-party services

Ad requests and related information are sent to Google Mobile Ads and may be processed by Google and relevant advertising service providers to provide the ad service. The app source reviewed does not show a publisher-operated service that receives gameplay records or account information. Google's processing is described in Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Help.

5. Advertising choices and consent

Google provides advertising and privacy controls through device and Google account settings; available controls can vary by location and device. The app currently sends standard ad requests and does not configure a non-personalized-ad request in the inspected code. Google says Android Advertising ID collection can be controlled through Android settings; Google's disclosure also describes App Set ID and, where applicable, account-related identifiers.

The project contains Google User Messaging Platform consent code, but its consent setting is currently disabled. As a result, the app does not currently run that consent flow before initializing or requesting ads. The publisher must review and configure the appropriate consent experience for the intended audience and distribution regions before release. See Google's AdMob privacy guidance for Flutter.

6. Retention and security

The app stores saved preferences in Android app storage. They may be removed when app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled; Android device backup or restore settings may also affect whether preferences are retained or restored. Google determines retention for information processed through its advertising services; consult Google's privacy resources for details. No specific retention period for Google's data is asserted here.

This app does not operate a publisher backend for the local game preferences described above. Information sent to Google is subject to Google's safeguards and practices. No security certification or guarantee is claimed by this policy.

7. Children

The reviewed app source does not contain an age gate or an in-app audience declaration. The publisher must confirm the intended age group and configure advertising and app distribution accordingly. Additional requirements may apply if the app is directed to, or knowingly used by, children.

8. Privacy rights and requests

Privacy rights and advertising choices depend on applicable Canadian federal and provincial laws. Where available, these rights may include requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or objecting to certain processing. The app currently does not provide an in-app privacy request or advertising opt-out form. Contact the publisher at lyambailukena2@gmail.com to submit a privacy request. Information processed by Google may also be subject to the controls and processes described by Google. You can remove locally saved app data through Android's app settings; doing so may delete saved game preferences and progress.

9. International processing

Google may process information in locations outside your country as described in its privacy materials. The app publisher does not operate a separate service that stores the local game preferences remotely.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app, its SDKs, or its data practices change. The current version and its effective date will be posted on this page.
